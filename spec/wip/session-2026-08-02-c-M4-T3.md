## Design (the hard part: a DETERMINISTIC SPATIAL fission source on the GPU)
- The eigen loop is the fission-SOURCE iteration: transport a generation's source,
  bank progeny at fission sites, the bank becomes the next generation's source;
  k iterates (E2a); entropy of the source converges (E2b, fixed 8³ mesh).
- Progeny are produced across a multi-superstep history, so per-collision banking
  needs per-particle progeny buffers or two passes. **Simplification that keeps k
  correct:** each source neutron RESERVOIR-samples ONE fission site (position +
  birth group from χ) weighted by per-collision production (from a separate
  fission stream, so the transport RNG is unperturbed), then banks
  ⌊production/k + ξ⌋ progeny there. k is insensitive to the exact spatial source
  (it converges regardless), so this gives the right eigenvalue; the 8³ entropy is
  approximate but adequate for E2b's convergence signal. Standard fission-site
  sampling.
- Determinism: reservoir + count from id-keyed streams; bank at prefix-sum slots
  (the M4-T2-b machinery); progeny streams by fork(parent, ctr, ordinal). The
  next generation transports the bank (each neutron by its stream). Bit-identical
  across configs.
