- **The nudge rule is the subtle part of `04 §4` and the easiest to get backwards.** Degeneracies (|discriminant| or |p| within ε=1e-9 cm of a boundary) nudge **along the direction of travel** (`p += ε·d`), then re-`locate`. MIN-14 is explicit that this is correct for BOTH entering and exiting crossings and that nudging unconditionally inward is wrong. Writing the test for both directions first.
