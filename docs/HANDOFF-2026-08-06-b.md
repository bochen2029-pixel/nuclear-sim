reflector). A more-accurate (more forward-peaked, less-moderating) Al would push the canonical
FURTHER from physical. Also: μ̄ (the big anisotropy lever, ADR-021's ~17000 pcm) is *already* correct
in the set. **The anisotropic-elastic transfer refinement is a small correctness improvement at best,
