- **The published models give ATOM DENSITIES (nuclei/b-cm), which is better than the wt% the spec's placeholders quote** — atom densities are what the transport consumes, so deriving wt%/ρ/mass from them makes every placeholder an independent cross-check rather than a restatement. All of them reproduce: Godiva 93.71/5.27/1.02 wt%, ρ 18.742, 52.43 kg; Jezebel Pu-240 4.47 wt%, Ga 1.02 wt%, ρ 15.611, 17.02 kg.

C-915 (natural Ga) stays `use=crosscheck`, readout-only.

- **"4.5% Pu-240" is unambiguous here even though sources disagree on the unit** — one search summary said at%, the spec says wt%. Computed from the atom densities it is **4.47 wt% and 4.50 at%**, so both readings round to the same figure. No amendment needed; recorded so nobody re-litigates it.
